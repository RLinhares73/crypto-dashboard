Method: ln(price) = a·ln(days since genesis) + c, least-squares fit to cycle extremes, same family as public log-regression / "rainbow" channel tools. Classic fit uses the 2011, 2013 & 2017 tops (a≈4.95); recalibrated adds the 2021 & 2025 tops (a≈4.38), which is what bends the ceiling down. Neither line passes through every top exactly — recalibrated misses 2017 by −36% and 2025 by +58%, since a straight log-log line can't fit 5 non-collinear points at once. Weighted fit uses the same 5 tops but with weights doubling each cycle (1, 2, 4, 8, 16 for 2011→2025), pulling the line toward recent history at the cost of the 2011 top (+130% miss) — 2025 is still off by +20%. Curved fit drops the straight-line assumption entirely and fits a quadratic, ln(price) = a·ln(days)² + b·ln(days) + c, which bends to track the accelerating deceleration — tightest overall fit, within ±17% on every top. Lower band is fit once to the 2011/2015/2018/2022 bottoms and held fixed across all versions; a sustained break of the lower band has not happened at this point in prior cycles. The 2013-04-28 → latest series is real daily-close data from CoinGecko, drawn at full daily resolution and extended automatically once a day. Exact cycle top/bottom dates (2013 onward) are real closes. The 2010–2013 stretch before CoinGecko's data start is a hand-reconstructed approximation (dotted line), and the 2011 top/bottom used in every fit above are approximate for the same reason (hollow markers). Fit constants were computed once and are NOT recalculated automatically; a new cycle top would require refitting them. Descriptive historical fit only; not a forecast or investment advice.
